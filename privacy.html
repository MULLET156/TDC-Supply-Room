Supply Room Price Helper: Privacy policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Supply Room Price Helper is a Chrome extension for dental offices that use the Supply Room inventory website. It reads product prices on the dental supplier websites listed below and saves them to the office's own Supply Room.

What it reads

  • On the supported supplier websites only: the product name, item number, pack size, price and list price shown on product pages the person opens, or that the extension opens in a background tab when the office's Supply Room asks for a price update.
  • Whether the page shows that someone is signed in (for example a "Sign out" link), so a signed-out list price is never saved as an account price.

What it does not read or collect

  • No passwords, sign-in details, payment details, cookies, browsing history or patient information.
  • Nothing on websites other than the supported supplier websites and the office's own Supply Room page.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.

Where the data goes

Prices are sent only to the Supply Room website the office pairs the extension with, using a pairing code from that Supply Room. They are not sent to the extension's developer or to any other party, and they are not sold or shared.

What is stored in the browser

The Supply Room's address and pairing key, the extension's settings, a short list of recently saved prices, and a copy of the office's product list (names, item numbers and supplier links) used to recognise product pages. Removing the extension deletes them.

Supported supplier websites

henryschein.com, pattersondental.com, benco.com, darbydental.com, safcodental.com, net32.com, frontierdental.com, dentalcity.com, ultradent.com, blueskybio.com, brasselerusa.com, burkhartdental.com, scottsdental.com, practicon.com, pearsondental.com, dhpsupply.com.
